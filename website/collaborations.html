What a collaboration is

The Atlas is the map. Collaborations are the map come true.

A few times a year I open one place on the Atlas for real: days in a master’s own workshop, with a small room chasing the same craft. All four, or it doesn’t run.

A named master

A real person with a record you can check, who teaches every day, in person.

Their own workshop

Their own lab, bench or kitchen, built for teaching.

Open enrolment

Anyone serious can take a chair. Nothing to win.

At the source

Where the craft is most alive, and the people are.

One rule about the numbers

They are published from day one.

Every collaboration states its minimum out loud. Enough chairs, it runs; if not, everyone is repaid in full. No invented scarcity, and a decision date that never moves.

You leave with the work in your hands, a certificate that says exactly what it should, and a cohort worth knowing.
