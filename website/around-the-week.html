Around the five days.

Lab Week 01 with Martín Lippo · Vakuum, Barcelona · 22–26 October 2026

First, I'd like to check whether you'd be interested in a few things around the week.

The lab takes the days, 10:00 to 14:00 and 15:00 to 18:00. Around it, I'd like to offer you a first dinner together on the 21st, the night before, and seven more things in and around Barcelona. Tick what you'd come to and I'll organise the ones the room wants. You pay each place directly.

Wednesday 21 October · the night before the lab

The first dinner

One table for all of us and one set menu, so the room meets properly. I've been in touch with all three and we'll go to the one that fits us best.

  • Ultramarinos MarínA counter for twelve facing a grill fired with holm oak, oak and olive wood
  • GrescaRafa Peña's game and offal, in half portions
  • Bar BrutalMore than 2,000 wines, dishes made to share

La BoqueriaJorge Franganillo · CC BY 2.0

A weekday morning · 08:00, at the lab for 10:00

La Boqueria, as it opens

A walk through the market while the stalls set up, and breakfast at one of its bars.

Worth knowingLate October is wild-mushroom season in Catalonia: look for rovellons and ceps on the stalls.

  • Mon–Sat, from 08:00
  • Free walk · you pay breakfast
  • boqueria.barcelona ↗

Vermut, olives, crispsAlzinous · CC BY-SA 3.0

An evening

A night of vermut and tapas

Vermut and small plates, the way Barcelona does it: from one bar to the next.

Worth knowingFer el vermut: vermouth from the tap, with olives, anchovies and crisps. In Barcelona it is a ritual, usually before Sunday lunch.

  • You pay as you go

Foie gras and smoked eel mille-feuillePhotograph · Martín Berasategui

Wednesday to Saturday night

Lasarte, three Michelin stars

Dinner in the Monument Hotel. Afterwards, we can go behind the scenes and have a look in the kitchen, with the chef.

Worth knowingIt is Martín Berasategui's three-star house in Barcelona, with Paolo Casagrande as head chef. His foie gras and smoked eel mille-feuille, first made in 1993, is on Lasarte's lunch menu.

  • Tasting menu €345, before wine
  • Closed Sunday to Tuesday
  • restaurantlasarte.com ↗

A pour of redJames Petts · CC BY-SA 2.0

An evening

A Catalan wine night

Catalan wines, and how to pair them with Spanish cooking, poured by a sommelier.

Worth knowingFrom Priorat reds grown on llicorella slate to Penedès xarel·lo, and which to pour with what.

Port Vell from MontjuïcJopsens · CC BY-SA 3.0

Sunday 25 or Monday 26 October · evening

Rice at Martínez, on Montjuïc

Rice dishes on the hill, with the city and the port below.

Worth knowingAsk for the arròs del senyoret, the 'gentleman's rice': every shell is off before it reaches you.

  • Open every day
  • Rice €24–30 a head
  • Full menu €62
  • 12 or more: group menus from €93
  • martinezbarcelona.com ↗

100%Chef, Vilassar de DaltPhotograph · 100%Chef

Tuesday 27 October · daytime

The 100%Chef showroom

The people who make tools and ingredients for modern cooking, at their lab in Vilassar de Dalt, about 25 km north-east of Barcelona.

Worth knowingTheir lab develops texturisers and tools for modern kitchens, and Martín's own site lists them as a partner.

  • Weekdays, by appointment
  • No charge
  • The ride there is ours to split
  • 100x100chef.com ↗

Recaredo's vineyard, Sant SadurníLanderBlazquez · CC BY-SA 4.0

Tuesday 27 October · daytime

Recaredo, in the Penedès

A family house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia. Two hours in the cellar, with a tasting.

Worth knowingIts sparkling wines age for years on their lees. In 2019 the house left the Cava name to join Corpinnat.

  • €42 a head
  • 2 hours, by appointment
  • 45 min from Barcelona by car
  • recaredo.com ↗
