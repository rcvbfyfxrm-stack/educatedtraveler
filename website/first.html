22–26 Oct · BarcelonaVakuum

Not only a class.

Five days at Martin Lippo's bench, and everything around it. This is what the week was.

In the lab

Martin's complete programme, run as one week in his own laboratory: textures, spherification, liquid nitrogen, foams and siphon, sous-vide. Thirty-five hours, every technique in your own hands.

All the techniques were actually very easy to do though they appeared very special.

Artem Karpenko, yacht chef on a 70m charter yacht

Around the lab

After the lab hours, the table: lunches together, a vermut and tapas night, dinners out in the city.

Not only do I feel like I’ve learned so much but also like I was traveling with a group almost.

Renata Sullivan, freelance yacht chef / private chef

The room

Yacht chefs, a private jet catering chef, private chefs. Everyone at the bench cooks for a living, and came to learn.

Everyone was there because they were truly interested in learning.

Jaimee Kivell, yacht chef

Wow wow wow what an incredible week shared with an incredible teacher, friends and city.

Alex, world traveling private chef
