Mixology & Bartending · Sesto San Giovanni (Milan), Italy

What we would publish

Teacher we would name: Giovanni Vecchio

  • Bar Training – Evolution: 40 hours in person at Campari Academy in Sesto San Giovanni, on the history of mixing, advanced product knowledge, ice, speed, and the drinks from punch and pre-Prohibition to tiki, modern classics and low-alcohol.
  • Each student has a bar station set up with professional equipment.
  • Ten places, €690. Next session 12 to 16 October 2026. Open to people who have not taken the Roots course first.
  • Taught by Giovanni Vecchio.
  • Campari Academy carries the name of the spirits producer Campari, and we would say so beside the entry.
