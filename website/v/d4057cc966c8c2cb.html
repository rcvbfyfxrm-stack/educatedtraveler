Japan Culinary Arts Academy

Fish butchery — ikejime and the Tsumoto method · Tokyo, Japan

We keep a free record of where crafts are still taught at the source. We would like to put you on it, and we would rather you saw it first. Nothing below is published yet.

Nobody pays to be on the record — no advertising, no commission, no booking fee — and sending us a correction will not move you up it.

What we would publish

We name no teacher — we could not establish one

  • A two-day course in the Tsumoto method and ikejime: day one is processing plus the science behind it, day two is rice, filleting for sushi, and finishing the dish for service.
  • Taught by professional Japanese chefs with interpreter support, for students who do not speak Japanese.

What we could not work out

  • What does the two-day course cost? We found a figure of USD 6,500 but it belongs to the four-week programme, and we will not attach it to this one.
  • When does it run?
  • Can we name the chef who teaches it?
  • How many students to a bench?

These are printed because they are the honest state of what we know. If you answer them we will say so, with the date.
