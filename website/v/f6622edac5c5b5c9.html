Jerry Thomas Educational — Campari Academy

Mixology & Bartending · Rome, Italy

We keep a free record of where crafts are still taught at the source. We would like to put you on it, and we would rather you saw it first. Nothing below is published yet.

Nobody pays to be on the record — no advertising, no commission, no booking fee — and sending us a correction will not move you up it.

What we would publish

Teacher we would name: Michele Garofalo

  • House of Classic: four days, 28 hours, on the history and technique of mixed drinks from the late 1700s to now — punch, sling and julep, Prohibition, tiki and the modern revival. €750 plus VAT. Next session 19 to 22 October 2026. Taught by Michele Garofalo.
  • Bar Lab & Innovation Program: five days, 35 hours, on bar techniques taken from modernist cooking and molecular pastry — acidifying and sweetening, clarifying and carbonating, smoking, dehydrating and freeze-drying, textures, fermentation, distillation and an ice programme. €850 plus VAT. Next session 30 November to 4 December 2026.
  • The Bar Lab is taught by the Jerry Thomas Educational trainers Michele Garofalo, Simone Onorati and Gregory Camillo.
  • Jerry Thomas Educational is the bartending school that grew out of the Jerry Thomas Speakeasy, and teaches these courses in Rome with Campari Academy. Campari Academy carries the name of the spirits producer Campari, and we would say so beside the entry.

What we could not work out

  • Is any course taught in English, or can it be followed with translation?
  • The Bar Lab page gives five days in one week and also says the course will last four months. Which is it?
  • How many places are there in each session?
