CoffeeMind Academy

Coffee & Barista · Hvidovre (Copenhagen), Denmark

We keep a free record of where crafts are still taught at the source. We would like to put you on it, and we would rather you saw it first. Nothing below is published yet.

Nobody pays to be on the record — no advertising, no commission, no booking fee — and sending us a correction will not move you up it.

What we would publish

Teacher we would name: Morten Münchow

  • A roasting and sensory school at Hvidovre, on the edge of Copenhagen, with six makes of roaster in the room: Aillio Bullet R1, Coffee-Tech Silon, Ikawa Pro, Loring 1 kg, Probatino and Stronghold S7 Pro.
  • SCA Roasting Intermediate: 5 to 7 January 2027, three days hands-on, €1,200, taught by Morten Münchow.
  • SCA Roasting Professional + Sensory Performance: 23 to 26 February 2027, €1,600, taught by Morten Münchow. The last run was six days — two of e-learning and four on site — with a sensory skills assessment taken from Ida Steen's research.
  • Morten Münchow was the lead creator of the SCA Roasting curriculum from 2013 to 2018.
  • CoffeeMind also sells e-learning courses; we would say which part of a course is on site and which is online.

What we could not work out

  • In what language are the on-site days taught?
  • How many students are in the room for each course?
  • Does the February 2027 course keep the six-day format, with two days online?

These are printed because they are the honest state of what we know. If you answer them we will say so, with the date.
