What we would publish

Teacher we would name: Luis Inchaurraga

  • A bar-school in Madrid, founded in 2012 by Luis Inchaurraga, bartender and author. Three courses run now: Cocktail Pairing, Barman Completo and Bar Management.
  • Cocktail Pairing: one intensive day in Madrid, at most 11 students, 11 tapas with 13 cocktails, €1,250. Luis Inchaurraga teaches it himself, and can teach it in English. Next dates: 4 November, 16 November and 7 December 2026.
  • The course is Luis Inchaurraga's own five-step method for pairing a cocktail with a dish: read the dish, decide what the pairing should do, find what links the two, build the cocktail, then taste it together and justify the result. It works from taste, aroma, texture and trigeminal sensations. It is written for bartenders, and also for chefs, private chefs and yacht chefs.
  • Barman Completo: five days, at most five students, €1,950, in English if you need it. Next dates: 19–23 October, 23–27 November and 14–18 December 2026.
  • Bar Management: the business side of a bar, from cost control and profit to running a team. At most 11 students, €1,250. Next dates: 15–16 October, 29–30 October and 2–3 December 2026.
  • The courses are held in what the school calls a real bar-school. It is a teaching bar, not open to the public, and the school's own words for the method are that you will not study theory in isolation.
