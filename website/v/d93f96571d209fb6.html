House of Mixology

Mixology & Bartending · Madrid, Spain

We keep a free record of where crafts are still taught at the source. We would like to put you on it, and we would rather you saw it first. Nothing below is published yet.

Nobody pays to be on the record — no advertising, no commission, no booking fee — and sending us a correction will not move you up it.

What we would publish

Teacher we would name: Luis Inchaurraga

  • A bar-school in Madrid, teaching bartenders since 2012.
  • Cocktail Pairing: five days in Madrid, at most 11 students, €1,250. The next date on the course page is 5 October 2026.
  • The course is Luis Inchaurraga's own system for pairing a cocktail with a dish, taught through six pairings created to explain it step by step — how aroma, taste, memory, context, trigeminal sensations and temperature work together.
  • Barman Completo: five days, at most five students, €1,950, with a start listed on 26 October 2026.
  • Both courses are held in what the school calls a real bar-school, and its own words for the method are that you will not study theory in isolation.

What we could not work out

  • Is the bar-school a bar that serves the public, or a teaching bar?
  • Is any course taught in English, or with translation?
  • Does Luis teach every day of Cocktail Pairing himself?
  • What are the 2027 dates?

These are printed because they are the honest state of what we know. If you answer them we will say so, with the date.
