&LAB TOKYO

Fermentation · Tokyo, Japan

We keep a free record of where crafts are still taught at the source. We would like to put you on it, and we would rather you saw it first. Nothing below is published yet.

Nobody pays to be on the record — no advertising, no commission, no booking fee — and sending us a correction will not move you up it.

What we would publish

Teacher we would name: Chie Ando

  • A four-rung course in koji and fermentation: Basic, Intermediate, Masterclass, and a fourth rung at which you teach it yourself.
  • Taught by Chie Ando, in English, and hands-on — students cultivate the koji themselves and make seasonings from it.
  • Prices are published: ¥39,500 Basic, ¥120,000 Intermediate, ¥120,000 Advanced, and ¥150,000 for a three-day Basic-plus-Intermediate intensive.

What we could not work out

  • Can a foreign professional chef enrol directly, or is there a prerequisite we have missed?
  • What are the dates, and how far ahead do they fill?
  • How many people are in a session?

These are printed because they are the honest state of what we know. If you answer them we will say so, with the date.
