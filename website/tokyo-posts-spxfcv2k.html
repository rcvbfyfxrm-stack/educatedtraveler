Three weeks in Tokyo with Hiroko Ishii, 1 to 19 March 2027: learn to serve an omakase in front of your guests.

In a restaurant, a whole team stands behind the sushi counter. On a boat or in a villa, there's only you.

Technique every day at her school in Otsuka: the rice, the yanagiba, a whole fish in three pieces, kombu-jime, vinegar cures, tamago, the rolls, shellfish. A tuna specialist breaks down a whole tuna, then you cut it. One Saturday morning at Toyosu market, ikejime in your own hands. In week three Hiroko serves a full omakase in front of you. On the last day you serve yours.

It is for professional chefs who have worked with raw fish and made sushi. €4,500, 8 seats. An €800 deposit saves yours. Flights and where you stay are not included.

Three chefs from the Circle have already taken their seat at Hiroko's bench. Five seats are left.

You can go direct: her courses are on jcma.tokyo. What we add is a course designed specially for experienced chefs, a room of working chefs, help finding a place to stay together, and everything around the lessons.

Take your seat, or talk to me first. The link is in the bio.
