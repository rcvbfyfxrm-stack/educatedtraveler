Five days at Martin Lippo's bench.

Barcelona, 22–26 October 2026. A room of working chefs, thirty-five hours in his own laboratory, in English. The week is over. This is what it was, in their words.

Collaboration 01 · Vakuum, Barcelona

The room.

Everyone at the bench cooks for a living: yacht chefs, a private jet catering chef, private chefs. Every application was read before a seat was confirmed. After the lab hours they ate and drank together — meals, a vermut and tapas night, dinners out. By the end, strangers were a group.

Nine of the chefs in the room, each with the photo they chose, shown because they said yes.

What the hands learned.

Martin's complete programme — the same five modules he sells separately — run as one consecutive week, 10:00–14:00 and 15:00–18:00. The foundations of the new culinary techniques and technologies, taught at professional pace.

Five days starts every technique in your own hands and leaves you the dossiers to work from afterwards. It does not hand you the craft — nothing five days long does.

Photographs from the five days at Vakuum.

Day one

Textures & texturizing agents

Crisps and puffed work, meringues, vegan creams and ice cream, gelatine sponges, broken gels, agar pearls and veils, bake-stable fillings, airs.

In service — garnishes that hold on a moving boat or a villa buffet, made days ahead.

Day two

Spherification — all of it

Theory, direct, reverse, multi, moulded, dehydrated, filled, frozen. Caviar technique and spherical gnocchi.

In service — the amuse-bouche that makes a charter or a dinner party, out of a small fridge.

Day three

Liquid nitrogen

Safety and storage first. Then nitro sorbets and shots, snows and powders, cryo-stamping, spheres and wafers, teppan work.

Straight talk — rarely practical at sea. Bank this day: dockside events, private houses, the years after boats.

Day four

Foams & siphon techniques

How foams stabilise; hot, cold, fat-based, egg-white and starch foams; microwave sponges; foams for frying; the Souflair.

In service — a siphon earns its locker space in any small kitchen.

Day five

Sous-vide, properly

Pasteurisation theory, core temperatures, direct and double cooking, long cooks, brining, conservation and regeneration.

In service — cook once on a quiet day, regenerate perfectly for twelve covers.

Who teaches it: Martin Lippo, Vakuum.

Argentine, born in Buenos Aires, in Barcelona since 2000. He never opened a restaurant. He built Vakuum instead — a laboratory and training centre whose whole purpose is teaching — and the Nitro School, because liquid nitrogen is the terrain he is known for. He works between Adrià's modernism and Escoffier's classicism. Don't take that from me — every line below is one click away:

  • His own laboratory, not a borrowed kitchen. Vakuum, Passatge de Centelles — built for teaching, and it opens to a serious stranger on a date.
  • Over sixty technique videos for Les Vergers Boiron. Filmed with him, free to watch — the library is here.
  • The Nitro School. A named liquid-nitrogen programme with its own students, not a party trick bolted onto a course.
  • His work carried by the trade press — so good.. magazine.
  • He teaches beyond his own room — tailored courses for hotels, caterers and schools, in Barcelona and abroad; the catalogue is on his site.
  • He knows your world. At twenty-six he crossed the Atlantic under sail — eighteen days at sea — and he has stood in the galley of a 70-metre superyacht.

The clearest evidence isn't a claim, it's who signs his reviews. On his own site, with names, roles and countries: the head pastry chef of Lasarte (three Michelin stars), the chef of Lluerna (one), an executive chef of the Lancaster group, a pastry director, an ice-cream maker, a mixology school director — and a working superyacht chef.
