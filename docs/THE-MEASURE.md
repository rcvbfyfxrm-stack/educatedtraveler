# The Measure

_How a place is judged on the Atlas. Five questions, the same five everywhere, answered
in public and signed by a person._

Arnaud, 7 September 2026: *"create a manifesto on how each place is evaluated. make it
clear, simple, so it's the standard for everything."*

This is that standard. Every grade already published was written against it, and every
grade written after it is refused if it breaks a rule below.

---

## The one idea

**A grade is not a score. It is five questions, each answered yes or no, with the working
shown.** A reader who disagrees with a dot can see exactly what it rests on and go and
check. That is the whole instrument, and everything below serves it.

There are no stars. There is no total out of ten. A craft with two dots is not worse than
one with four — it is a craft where three of the five questions could not be answered yes,
and the page says which three and why.

---

## The five questions

They are fixed, in this order, on every craft. They live in code
(`scripts/atlas_hub.py` → `MEASURE_QUESTIONS`) and the build refuses a grade that asks
its own.

1. **Is there a teacher, and can we name them?**
2. **Can a stranger get in, on a date you can book?**
3. **Will there be other people learning beside you?**
4. **Is the craft alive in this place, or is the school the only thing here?**
5. **Is there enough here to keep you learning for years?**

They were nouns once — the room, the door, the bench, the ground, the stretch — and that
failed on the page: a reader met *"The stretch."* above a paragraph that never said what a
stretch was. A legend is only learnable if it is a plain question.

---

## What lights a dot

### 1 · The teacher

Two ways in, and only two.

- **A named person who actually teaches the course**, on a page anyone can open.
- **An examiner outside the school** — a state diploma, a university credit, a trade body
  — who sets the standard for **this course** and marks against it.

That is it. In particular:

- **A founder is not a teacher.** Founding a school, directing it, or having your name over
  the door is not teaching in it. This rule cost French pastry a dot (Frédéric Bau,
  "founder and creative director"), freediving a dot (three founders), and goldsmithing a
  dot (Gio Carbone, "in the school he founded").
- **A famous name attached to a school is not a teacher.** Pierre Hermé and Cédric Grolet
  were carried against FERRANDI until FERRANDI told us in writing that neither has any
  connection to their training. Jiro Ono was carried against Tokyo sushi schools he teaches
  at none of.
- **A dead person is not a teacher**, however much the craft owes them.
- **Reputation, age, size and alumni count for nothing.** Every "well-established" signal we
  hold is generated by our own catalogue, so a dot lit from one is the house grading itself.
- **The school we recommend is the one that has to answer.** If the pick cannot name a
  teacher, this dot is dark even when another destination on the same craft can. The reader
  is going to the pick.

⭐ **Names are a plus-value, not the criterion.** Always publish a name when one exists —
but in the right field. `masters` is who teaches now; `lineage` is where the craft came
through. **A name is never deleted for sitting in the wrong field. It is moved.**

### 2 · The door

Published dates, a price, and a way in that does not require a CV. A stage you apply for is
not a course you can book, and the page must say which it is.

And the hands have to be able to ruin it. If nothing you do in ten days can be wrong in a
way somebody could show you, you are watching a demonstration, not learning a craft.

### 3 · The bench

Other people, learning, at the same time. A published cohort fact — a class size, a group
cap, a residential format. Our own community ranking is not evidence for this; it is our
opinion, and one instrument may not vouch for another.

### 4 · The ground

Is the craft alive in the place, or has a school been parachuted in? This dot needs
**evidence from somebody with nothing to sell you**: a festival, a guild register, a market,
a trade body, a state listing — with the URL and the day it was read. A school's own page is
never evidence about its town. The build refuses this dot without it.

### 5 · The stretch

Is there a rung above the course, set by somebody outside the school? A certificate of
attendance proves attendance. A state diploma, a university degree, a trade qualification
with an examiner is a ladder.

⚠ Watch for the closed loop: a body that teaches the method, defines the method and
certifies the teachers of the method is not an outside standard. Said of the Wim Hof Method
Academy on its own page, and true wherever it is true.

## What a dark dot means

Exactly one of three things, and the sentence under it says which:

1. **Nobody publishes the answer** — ten kite schools, not one named instructor.
2. **We read it and the answer is no** — the certificate is issued by the school that
   teaches it.
3. **Nobody of ours has been** — an admission about us, not a finding about the place.

A dark dot is not automatically a criticism, and it is not automatically an excuse. The page
may not blur the two: for eight weeks the legend claimed an empty dot was never a mark
against a place, while four dark dots on the map were exactly that.

---

## The rules that hold it up

**A school cooperating with us never moves its grade.** Not by replying, not by sending
photographs, not by being pleasant. Thirty-two schools were told this in writing in
September 2026, so it has to be true afterwards. When Atmavikasa answered a direct question
and named its teachers, hatha yoga's grade stayed at two — because the recommended room in
Rishikesh still named nobody.

**Nobody pays to be on the Atlas, and no money changes what it says.** Where a commercial
relationship exists it is disclosed at the point of the judgement, not in a footer.

**A grade carries a person's name and the day it was signed.** Drafts live in
`data/atlas-measure-drafts.json`, which the build never reads, so a draft cannot reach the
site by accident. Signing is `scripts/sign-measure.py <craft> --as "Name"` — the name is
typed by the person whose name it is, one craft at a time. No script may write a `checker`.

**A grade is amended in the open.** Correcting published text means saying what changed and
when, in the basis line. A signed judgement rewritten silently is worse than the error.

**The evidence has to be checkable, or the dot does not light.** A URL and the date it was
read. "We were told" is not evidence. A name in an email is not evidence.

---

## What caps it

A desk grade cannot reach the top. Nobody from EducatedTraveler has stood in most of these
rooms, and the instrument says so rather than pretending. The fifth question has never been
lit on any craft, because the ladder can be read but the climb cannot.

⭐ **Decided 5 September 2026, not yet shipped:** "have we been" becomes its own sixth
question, so it stops darkening the other five. A place can then show five of six honestly,
and no craft on the map can show a full meter until somebody goes.
